NIMBUS NOTES - PRODUCT FAQ

What is Nimbus Notes?
Nimbus Notes is a note-taking and knowledge-sharing app for teams, made by Nimbus Labs. It runs
on Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS and Android, and in any modern web browser.

What plans are available and how much do they cost?
There are three plans. The Free plan costs nothing and includes 2 GB of storage per user.
The Pro plan costs 12 USD per user per month (or 120 USD per user per year when billed
annually) and includes 100 GB of storage per user. The Enterprise plan has custom pricing,
unlimited storage, single sign-on (SSO) and a dedicated account manager.

Is there a free trial?
Yes. Every new workspace gets a 14-day free trial of the Pro plan. No credit card is required
to start the trial.

What is the refund policy?
Pro subscriptions can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, no refunds
are given for the current billing period. Enterprise contracts are not refundable, but unused
months can be credited towards a renewal.

Does Nimbus Notes work offline?
Yes. Since version 2.3, notes are available offline on desktop and mobile apps. Changes made
offline are synced automatically when the device reconnects.

How do I contact support?
Free plan users can use the community forum. Pro users can email support@nimbusnotes.example
and receive a reply within 24 hours on business days. Enterprise customers get 24/7 phone
support with a 1-hour response time for critical issues.

Can I export my notes?
Yes. Notes can be exported to Markdown, PDF or HTML at any time from Settings > Export. Exports
include attachments.

How many people can edit a note at the same time?
Up to 50 people can edit the same note simultaneously with real-time collaboration.
