# Nimbus Labs Employee Handbook

Nimbus Labs is a fictional software company that builds Nimbus Notes, a note-taking app.
This handbook summarises the policies that apply to all full-time employees.

## Working Hours

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local time zone. Outside core
hours, employees are free to organise their own schedule, provided they work an average of
40 hours per week.

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Tuesdays and Thursdays are office days for
all teams, so that planning meetings can happen in person. Fully remote arrangements need
written approval from the department head.

## Paid Time Off

Every full-time employee receives 25 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year, in addition
to public holidays. Up to 5 unused PTO days can be carried over into the next year; any
remaining days expire on 31 March. PTO requests of more than 5 consecutive days must be
submitted at least 2 weeks in advance.

## Parental Leave

Nimbus Labs offers 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave to all parents, including adoptive
parents. Parental leave can be taken in up to two blocks within the first year after birth or
adoption.

## Learning Budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,500 USD for courses, books and conferences.
Unused budget does not roll over to the next year.

## Expenses

Travel and meal expenses must be submitted in the Expensify tool within 30 days of the purchase.
Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 60 USD per day. Any single expense above
500 USD requires manager pre-approval.

## Onboarding

Every new hire is assigned an onboarding buddy from a different team for their first 90 days.
The buddy meets the new hire at least once per week and helps them navigate the company.
